# Phase 2d: the sixteen documents of Vatican II in the *Acta Apostolicae Sedis*

Generated by `tools/acta-conciliar-report.ts` from the committed fixtures, `tools/src/acta/curation.ts` and `data/`. It reads no volume store, so CI regenerates it. Spec: [acta volumes design §12](../specs/2026-09-13-acta-volumes-design.md).

## 1. The hole this phase closed, and the ruling that closed it

The corpus carries a reference on **6,991 of its 8,569** records (81.6 %). Before this phase the sixteen `oec:vatican-ii` records carried none, and they were the only issuer at zero a gazette could reach. The issuers that remain at zero are `rp:benedict-xiv` (43 records) and `rp:leo-xiv` (40 records) — Benedict XIV's records predate the *Acta Sanctae Sedis*, which begins in 1865, and Leo XIV's postdate the printed AAS.

The AAS files the council's documents in **a part of its own**, which `index.ts` reads as a part (`PART_HEADING_RE` matches any heading opening `ACTA`) and then skips, `POPE_PART_RE` failing on `SS.` — as it skips the dicasteries'. Phase 2c-ii-d had already ruled on why they should carry a reference all the same: a conciliar act printed under the pope who promulgated it *is the same document*, which is why *Dei Filius* and *Pastor Aeternus* are cited on their `oec:vatican-i` records (ass volumes spec §5). Every one of these sixteen carries `promulgatedBy: rp:paul-vi`, the registry itself asserting what that ruling asserts.

Measured from the parse result's own `skippedParts` over the 116 non-ASS sources of `ACTA_SOURCES`: **107 distinct skipped part headings**, of which **3 are the council's**, over 4 sources.

| `III - ACTA Ss. OECUMENICI CONCILII` | 1964 |

The heading wraps in 3 of the four (1964, 1965, 1966): there the parser keeps only its first line, which is why `VATICANI II` is absent from those rows of the tally, while 1962 sets it on one line and the tally carries it whole. **4 further headings** belong to the synod of bishops, a different body, over 8 sources: `II - SYNODUS EPISCOPORUM` (1977), `II – ACTA SYNODI EPISCOPORUM` (2014, 2015, 2019, 2023, 2024), `II. – ACTA SYNODI EPISCOPORUM` (2018), `XIV - ACTA SYNODALIA` (1980).

Those four parts hold **18 entries** — AAS 54 1, AAS 56 2, AAS 57 3, AAS 58 12 — of which **16 are the registry's**. A rule for 18 entries in 4 volumes cannot grow, the council having closed in 1965, and it would need a title axis the matcher has nowhere else (the part files by title, never by incipit), a line that prints no date at all, and a line the OCR fuses. That trade has been refused twice before on measurement — the general `promulgatedBy` rule, built and measured over all 41 ASS volumes to an identical match set, and `LITERAE`, which read 0 acts over the same 41 — so the references are curated and the accounting is taken here, where the lines are read.
